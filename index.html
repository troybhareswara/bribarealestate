Our Story

We are a mixed Indonesian family business, and our three beautiful villas in the heart of Bali have been welcoming guests for over 20 years. in 2023, we completed a beautiful renovation — blending modern comfort with the soul of traditional Balinese architecture. Today, our vintage villas are available for daily rental through Airbnb, and we couldn't be more excited to share this piece of paradise with you.

For us, service and comfort are not just priorities — they are our passion. From airport pickups to birthday decorations, we handle every detail so you can simply relax and enjoy your stay. Just ask, and we'll do our best to make it happen.
